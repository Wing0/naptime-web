Privacy Policy — Naptime Free

Effective Date: July 11, 2026

This policy applies only to the free, donation-supported edition of Naptime ("Naptime Free"). If you use the paid edition of Naptime, that app collects and transmits no data and is covered by our standard Privacy Policy instead.

Naptime Free is offered to you at no cost. In exchange, you agree to contribute pseudonymous data about app use, sleep sessions, and app stability, which we may use as described below. This is the trade that keeps the free edition free. The data is not linked to an account or direct identity fields, but it is linked over time by a random installation identifier and is therefore not anonymous. This policy explains what we collect, the resulting risks, what we do with it, and your deletion options.

1. Your Consent

When you first open Naptime Free, you are shown a screen explaining what the app collects, that the data is pseudonymous, and that it may be shared with research partners and other third parties. Agreeing to these data-donation terms is required to use the free edition — no data is uploaded until you have agreed, but the free edition cannot be used without agreeing. If you prefer not to share your data, you can use the paid edition of Naptime instead, which keeps all session data on your device and uploads nothing.

Using Naptime Free with data contribution enabled constitutes your agreement that we may collect, store, analyze, and use the pseudonymous data described in this policy, without further compensation to you, for the purposes and retention period described below.

2. Website Analytics and Ad Measurement

On the Naptime website, we may use Google Analytics and Reddit Pixel after cookie consent to understand website traffic, landing-page performance, campaign parameters, and clicks on download or navigation links. This website measurement is separate from Naptime Free app data donation.

3. What We Collect in the App

Sleep & Nap Session Data

When you complete a nap or an overnight sleep session and have consented, the app uploads a summary of that session. This includes:

  • Timing: session start and wake-up times, total duration, the length and position of your longest continuous sleep, and any sleep/wake segments detected.
  • Movement statistics: aggregated, time-windowed summaries of your phone's motion sensor readings (e.g. high/low/median movement per minute, percentiles, burst scores). We do not upload raw, continuous accelerometer streams — only these derived statistics.
  • Sleep quality signals: detected wake events, micro-arousals/twitches, sleep efficiency, snooze events, and any star/emoji rating or manual corrections you make to a session.
  • Tags: names of automatic, selected, or custom tags attached to the session. Because custom tag names are uploaded as entered, do not put names or other identifying details in them.
  • Session settings snapshot: the relevant app settings in effect for that session (e.g. alarm sound, sensitivity, nap duration, wake-up time, tracking toggles, Do Not Disturb status).
  • Device metadata: your device manufacturer, model, Android version, and SDK level, plus whether the build was a debug build.

Stability & Crash Data

To keep the app reliable, we upload technical records of crashes and abnormal process exits. These may include the exact exit time, reason and description, stack trace, memory/process status, recent in-app event breadcrumbs, whether a nap or sleep session was active, that session's start time, app version, and the device metadata listed above. Diagnostics are linked to the same installation identifier.

Installation & Security Identifiers

The app creates a random installation identifier so records from one installation can be grouped, plus a separate random per-install upload credential that authorizes uploads and deletion requests. Neither is derived from your hardware or account. The server stores one-way hashes of these values rather than the raw values.

Before accepting production contributions, the app uses Google Play Integrity to check that enrollment comes from the Play-recognized Naptime Free app, a licensed Play installation, and a device meeting Google's integrity requirements. Google processes the app package/version/signing information, Play license status, a hash binding the enrollment request, and device-integrity signals for this check. We store only the returned verdict categories and verification timestamps, not a Google account identifier or the encrypted integrity token.

Operational Network Security

Like any internet service, our server necessarily processes the source network address to deliver a response and prevent abuse. We do not include the raw address in contributed session or crash records and have disabled application access logging. Rate limiting stores only a salted one-way hash of the address or network group. An inactive bucket becomes eligible for removal after five minutes and is removed by later traffic or hourly cleanup; a service outage may delay cleanup.

What We Do NOT Collect

  • No account, name, email address, or phone number.
  • No advertising ID, Google account identifier, hardware serial, or account-based identifier. The app does create the random installation identifier described above.
  • No location data, contacts, microphone audio, or content from other apps.
  • No raw accelerometer recordings.
  • No in-app analytics, advertising, or third-party crash-reporting SDKs. Session and diagnostic contributions go only to our servers. The Google Play Integrity security library communicates with Google only for the installation-verification check described above.

4. Pseudonymisation and Identification Risk

Naptime Free reduces direct identification but does not make contributed records anonymous:

  • No account or direct identity fields are required. You never create an account, and we do not ask for your name, email address, phone number, advertising ID, or location.
  • Data is keyed only by a random installation identifier. The first time data is uploaded, the app generates a random identifier (a UUID) for that installation. This number is not derived from your hardware, your Google account, or any advertising ID, and it is generated freshly if you reinstall or reset your data. It lets us group sessions from the same installation (for example, to measure whether sleep improves over time) without knowing who you are.
  • Indirect identification remains possible. Exact session and diagnostic times, device/OS details, settings, tags, and repeated records can distinguish an installation and could be combined with information held elsewhere. We do not claim that re-identification is impossible.

Until the installation link has been irreversibly removed and the records have been aggregated so that individuals are no longer reasonably distinguishable, we treat the contributed dataset as pseudonymous data, not anonymous data.

5. How We Use the Data — and Our Right to Exploit It

In exchange for providing Naptime Free at no charge, you grant us a worldwide, royalty-free right to use the pseudonymous records while we lawfully retain them, and to use irreversibly anonymized aggregate results, for any lawful purpose. This expressly includes, without limitation:

  • Improving Naptime's sleep-detection algorithms, features, and reliability.
  • Performing research and statistical analysis on sleep and rest.
  • Building, training, and validating models and other products derived from the data.
  • Producing and publishing aggregated insights, benchmarks, and reports.
  • Sharing, licensing, selling, or otherwise commercializing pseudonymous records or irreversibly anonymized aggregate results with third parties, including partners and researchers.

Your deletion rights for records still linked to the installation identifier are described in Section 7. Information that was irreversibly anonymized and aggregated before deletion is no longer associated with that identifier and cannot be separated back into an individual contribution.

6. Where the Data Is Stored & Who Processes It

Uploaded data is transmitted over an encrypted HTTPS connection and stored on servers we operate. A random per-install credential authorizes uploads and deletion requests; the server stores its one-way SHA-256 hash rather than the raw credential. Google Play Integrity additionally verifies production enrollment as described in Section 3; it does not identify the user to us. HTTPS protects the credential and payload in transit.

Our servers are hosted in European data centres operated by Hetzner Online GmbH, which acts as our hosting provider. Hetzner stores the data on our behalf but has no independent right to use it. Google processes the limited integrity-check information described in Section 3 as our app-security provider. Research partners or other third parties may receive pseudonymous records or aggregate results as described in Section 5.

7. Your Right to Retract Your Data

You can request deletion of the pseudonymous records associated with your installation identifier. Local and server-side deletion work differently:

  • From your device: Tapping "Delete my data" permanently erases local sleep records, session history, crash logs, tags, settings, and consent state. If server deletion has not yet been confirmed, the app temporarily keeps the random installation identifier and a deletion credential in app-private storage excluded from backup and device transfer. A network-constrained background job uses them only to retry deletion, then removes them after confirmation. Keep Naptime installed and connect it to the internet until confirmation; uninstalling first removes the retry credential and may prevent the app from completing server deletion.
  • From our servers: The same "Delete my data" action sends a deletion request for sessions, crash logs, and metadata associated with your installation identifier. The server deletes all linked session, crash, app, and device metadata once it successfully receives and accepts that request. A working connection is required; contact us if you need help confirming server-side deletion. Minimal one-way credential/installation hashes and deletion-request status and timestamps are normally removed within 24 hours solely to make retries safe and prevent a deleted credential from being reused; a service outage may delay that cleanup.

Using "Delete my data" is also how you stop future contributions: it clears your consent, so the app stops uploading and will ask for your consent again before any further data is contributed. Note that data which has already been irreversibly aggregated or anonymized into a pooled dataset before your retraction may no longer be separable, and to that extent cannot be individually withdrawn.

If you would like assistance retracting your data, contact us using the details below.

8. Data Sharing

We do not collect account-based identity fields. We may share, license, or sell pseudonymous records or irreversibly anonymized aggregate results as described in Section 5. Sharing pseudonymous records can increase indirect identification risk.

9. Children's Privacy

Naptime Free is not directed to children under the age of digital consent in their jurisdiction. We do not knowingly collect data from such children. Because the app has no account or age field, we generally cannot determine a user's age; if you believe a child has used the app, you may request deletion as described in Section 7.

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the effective date above.
